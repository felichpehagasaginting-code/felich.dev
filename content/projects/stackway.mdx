### Problem & Motivation
Technical documentation and computer science concepts are frequently shrouded in dense jargon, making it difficult for beginners to grasp underlying principles.

### Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Platform Stack**: Built using **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**.
- **Interactive Curricula**: Features 6 structured learning tracks covering frontend, backend, AI/ML basics, and system architecture explained through accessible real-world mental models.
