### Technical Architecture
- **Framework**: **Next.js 16 App Router** with **TypeScript** and **Tailwind CSS v3**.
- **Backend Integrations**: Lazy-loaded **Firebase SDK** for real-time guestbook entries, visitor count analytics, and interactive contact submissions.
- **UI State & Accessibility**: **Zustand** store persisted to local storage for instant layout and theme toggling across 4 curated themes.
