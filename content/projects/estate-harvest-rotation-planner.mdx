### Problem & Motivation
In large-scale palm oil plantations, daily harvest rotation decisions are traditionally made using manual intuition. Suboptimal rotation scheduling leads to overripe fruit loss, unharvested blocks, and decreased oil extraction rates.

### Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Mobile Client**: Developed with **Flutter & Dart**, providing a fast, cross-platform mobile user experience tailored for field operational managers.
- **Priority Algorithm**: Evaluates historical block rotation intervals, ripening rates, rainfall metrics, and worker capacity to calculate daily mathematical priority scores for each plantation block.
- **Offline Architecture**: Designed with offline-first data caching to ensure field managers can record data even in remote plantation zones without cellular connectivity.
