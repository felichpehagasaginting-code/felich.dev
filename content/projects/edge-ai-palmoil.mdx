### Problem & Motivation
Grading oil palm Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) at palm oil mills is historically done manually by inspectors, creating inconsistency in quality ratings and vulnerability to human bias.

### Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Edge Inference**: Deploys lightweight convolutional neural networks directly onto the **Analog Devices / Maxim MAX78000** microcontroller. The CNN performs sub-second image classification of palm fruit ripeness at ultra-low power consumption.
- **Gateway & Failover**: Connected via an **ESP-12E gateway** equipped with dual-protocol failover (MQTT over Wi-Fi / Cellular with automatic fallback to LoRa for long-range remote plantation transmission).
- **Data & Dashboard**: Ingests telemetry streams into **TimescaleDB** time-series database and displays live operational metrics, ripeness distributions, and sensor logs on a real-time web dashboard.
