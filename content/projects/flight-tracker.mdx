### Problem & Motivation
Airline ticket pricing algorithms frequently fluctuate based on booking velocity and demand surges. Manually monitoring flight fares between Medan (KNO) and Jakarta (CGK) to secure optimal pricing is time-consuming and inefficient.

### Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Scraping Engine**: Developed using **Node.js** and **Playwright headless browser automation** to navigate Google Flights DOM structures, handle dynamic SSR loading, and extract live flight fare data without triggering anti-bot protections.
- **Notification Pipeline**: Integrates directly with the **Telegram Bot API** via webhook payload dispatch. When a ticket price drops below a pre-configured price threshold, instant notifications with flight timing, airline provider, and direct booking URLs are pushed to the user's mobile device.
- **Scheduling**: Driven by background cron tasks for periodic execution at scheduled intervals.
